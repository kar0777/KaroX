## How this support is used

Support is directed first toward active KaroX development and integration work, including:

- provider adapters and streaming compatibility;
- tool-calling and structured-output behavior;
- retry safety and duplicate-side-effect prevention;
- permission boundaries, secret filtering, and destructive-action safeguards;
- tests, browser verification, screenshots, and evidence-backed completion;
- controlled public or synthetic evaluations after the implementation is stable.

KaroX is deeply grateful to every person who chose to help a young open-source developer keep building.
