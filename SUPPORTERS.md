# The people and teams helping KaroX grow

### Built by one student — strengthened by an open-source community

## How support is used

Support is directed first toward active KaroX implementation and integration work: provider adapters and protocol compatibility; local and hosted-agent paths; tool calling and evidence collection; retry safety; permission and secret boundaries; browser verification; cross-platform reliability; and controlled evaluations after the implementation is stable.

KaroX is grateful to every person who chose to help a young open-source developer keep building. Each grant, account upgrade, technical answer, license, credit, and encouraging message reduced a real barrier between an idea and a working, testable system.
