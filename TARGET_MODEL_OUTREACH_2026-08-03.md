## Status legend

- `SENT_NEW` — first verified outbound message to this exact company/address.
- `REPLIED` — substantive human response received.
- `APPROVED_ACTION_REQUIRED` — support approved, but activation or verification remains.
- `RECEIVED_ACTIVE` — credits/plan/model access is visible and usable.
- `REFUSED` — the company explicitly declined.
- `STANDARD_ROUTE` — redirected to an ordinary public plan/program without special support.
- `NO_RESPONSE` — no substantive reply after the chosen follow-up window.
- `BOUNCED` — delivery failed.
